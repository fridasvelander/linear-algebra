 KEEP THIS HANDY

A quick change of perspective.

01 / Bases

A basis of ℝ² is a pair of linearly independent vectors. Put them in the columns of a matrix B. They form a basis exactly when det(B) ≠ 0.

02 / Coordinates

Coordinates describe how much of each basis vector you need. If [v]ᴮ = (c₁, c₂), then v = c₁b₁ + c₂b₂. To find them, solve Bc = v.

03 / Change of basis

To go from B-coordinates to C-coordinates, use [v]ᶜ = C⁻¹B[v]ᴮ. The vector stays the same; only its description changes.
